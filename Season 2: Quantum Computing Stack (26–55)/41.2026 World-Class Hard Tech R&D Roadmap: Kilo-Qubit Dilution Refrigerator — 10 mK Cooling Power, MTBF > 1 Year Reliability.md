## Abstract

Scaling superconducting quantum processors to the kilo-qubit regime demands a dilution refrigerator capable of both high cooling power at the 10 mK base stage and continuous unattended operation for over one year. Current commercial single-unit dilution refrigerators deliver 400–800 μW at 100 mK and 20–30 μW at 20 mK, with MTBF typically below 6 months due to compressor wear and intermittent valve failures. This roadmap specifies a **10 mK-stage cooling power sufficient to sustain > 1000 qubits with full control/readout wiring**, targeting **> 1500 μW at 100 mK and > 40 μW at 20 mK from a single mixing chamber**, paired with a **system-level MTBF > 8760 hours (> 1 year)** through oil-free compressor architecture, redundant valve manifolds, and predictive condition monitoring. All components are selected from COTS industrial-grade cryogenic and vacuum equipment. No proprietary gas mixtures or custom-built compressors are required. Performance is verified using standard helium-3/helium-4 circulation tests and vibration-compensated temperature logging.

## 1. Pain Point Definition (Why)

Current 60-score baseline: Commercial single-unit dilution refrigerators (Bluefors/Kelvinox class) provide 400–800 μW at 100 mK and 20–30 μW at 20 mK. Kilo-qubit systems with 2000+ DC/microwave lines, cryogenic HEMTs, and circulators impose a static + dynamic heat load exceeding 1200 μW at 100 mK and 35 μW at 20 mK. This forces operators to either run two parallel units (doubling footprint and failure modes) or accept base-temperature drift > 5 mK during active gating—both unacceptable for error-corrected logical qubits.

- **Failure Mode 1:** Cooling power ceiling. Single-unit designs hit a wall at ~800 μW@100 mK. Beyond this, adding more ³He circulation requires larger pumps, longer still lines, and higher compressor throughput—each introducing new vibration and thermal anchoring failure points.
- **Failure Mode 2:** MTBF cliff. Oil-sealed compressors require oil changes every 4000–6000 hours. Diaphragm fatigue in the ³He/⁴He circulation pumps causes 60% of unplanned downtime events. Vibration-induced qubit decoherence is a secondary but chronic failure mode.
- **Failure Mode 3:** Service interruption cost. A single unexpected shutdown costs 3–5 days of rewarming, repair, and recooling. At MTBF < 6 months, this translates to > 10% annual uptime loss for a production quantum computer.

**The 60-score solution has exhausted all adjustable parameter degrees of freedom — further tuning degrades efficiency, further modification requires different hardware. Its ceiling is not a technical limitation; it is a physical one.**

## 2. Breakthrough Solution (What)

**Core Architecture:** Single-unit high-power dilution refrigerator with oil-free scroll compressor train, dual-redundant ³He circulation pumps, and vibration-isolated pulse-tube pre-cooling.

## 1. 痛点定义（为什么）

## 1. Problemdefinition (Warum)

Aktuelle 60-Punkte-Baseline: Kommerzielle Einzel-Einheit-Verdünnungskryostate (Bluefors/Kelvinox-Klasse) liefern 400–800 μW bei 100 mK und 20–30 μW bei 20 mK. Kilo-Qubit-Systeme mit über 2000 DC-/Mikrowellenleitungen, kryogenen HEMTs und Zirkulatoren erzeugen eine statische + dynamische Wärmebelastung von über 1200 μW bei 100 mK und 35 μW bei 20 mK. Dies zwingt Bediener entweder zum Betrieb von zwei parallelen Einheiten (Verdopplung von Stellfläche und Ausfallmodi) oder zur Akzeptanz einer Basis-Temperaturdrift von > 5 mK während aktiver Gattersteuerung – beides inakzeptabel für fehlerkorrigierte logische Qubits.

- **Fehlermodus 1:** Kälteleistungs-Deckel. Einzel-Einheit-Designs stoßen bei ~800 μW@100 mK an eine Wand. Darüber hinaus erfordert das Hinzufügen von mehr ³He-Zirkulation größere Pumpen, längere Still-Leitungen und höheren Kompressordurchsatz – jeweils mit neuen Vibrations- und thermischen Anker-Ausfallpunkten.
- **Fehlermodus 2:** MTBF-Klippe. Ölgedichtete Kompressoren erfordern alle 4000–6000 Stunden einen Ölwechsel. Membranermüdung in den ³He/⁴He-Zirkulationspumpen verursacht 60% der ungeplanten Ausfallzeiten. Vibrationsinduzierte Qubit-Dekohärenz ist ein sekundärer, aber chronischer Ausfallmodus.
