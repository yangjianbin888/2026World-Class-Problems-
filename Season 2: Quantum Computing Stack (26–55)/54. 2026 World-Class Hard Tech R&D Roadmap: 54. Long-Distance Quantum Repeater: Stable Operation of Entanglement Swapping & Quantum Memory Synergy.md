**0. Pain Point Definition (Why)**
*   **Memory-Coherence Bottleneck:** Rare-earth-ion or semiconductor quantum memories suffer from < 10 ms dephasing (T₂) at room temperature or cryogenic without dynamical decoupling, limiting storage time to < 1 km light travel equivalent, making multi-hop swapping impossible without frequent refresh losses.
*   **Swap-Asynchrony Chaos:** Independent nodes lack a unified clock domain; probabilistic BSM successes arrive at neighboring nodes with random latencies. Without feed-forward buffers and time-tagging, successful swaps are lost due to memory-readout mistiming > 50% of the time.
*   **Heralding Latency Mismatch:** Photon arrival at BSM stations triggers a heralding signal, but the round-trip-time (RTT) over 500 km fiber (~5 ms) exceeds memory coherence or control-system reaction time, causing premature memory decay before the swap is confirmed.
*   **Fidelity Accumulation:** Each entanglement swap theoretically costs a factor of η ≈ 0.5 in fidelity. Without purification or high-initial-fidelity (F > 0.99) sources, 10-hop chains see fidelity drop to < 0.5^10 ≈ 0.001%.

    *   **White-Rabbit (WR) Grandmaster:** A WR-PTP grandmaster clock at the central node distributes < 100 ps synchronized timestamps to all repeater nodes via fiber. This creates a single time-domain where swap events and memory-read windows are globally scheduled.

*   **F:** "Festkörperspeicher wie Y₂SiO₅ erfordern 4K-Kryogenik – das ist nicht COTS-rack-montierbar."
