# 2026 World-Class Hard Tech R&D Roadmap: 50. Device-Independent DI-QKD: Complete Detection Loophole Closure & Rigorous Bell Inequality Verification

**Abstract**
This document defines an engineering roadmap for Device-Independent Quantum Key Distribution (DI-QKD) with full loophole closure and rigorous Bell inequality verification over metropolitan-scale distances (≥ 100 km fiber). The baseline (60 score) is fair-sampling DI-QKD relying on the detection loophole assumption, limited to lab-scale distances (< 10 m) with unclosed locality and measurement-dependence loopholes. The proposed solution (90 score) integrates **heralded entanglement swapping + ≥ 90% SNSPD detection efficiency + ≥ 2 km space-like separation + quantum-proof random setting choice** to achieve a complete loophole-free Bell test with CHSH value S ≥ 2.3, supporting finite-size-secure key generation at ≥ 0.05 bits/pair under the Entropy Accumulation Theorem (EAT). The design enforces COTS-grade single-photon detectors, off-the-shelf fiber infrastructure, and reserves explicit redundancy (virtual axis) for on-site calibration.

**0. Pain Point Definition (Why)**
*   **Detection Loophole (Fair Sampling):** Most DI-QKD demonstrations assume fair sampling—only detected events are analyzed, implicitly trusting that undetected events would follow the same statistics. This opens a side channel for an adversary to bias the sample without triggering protocol abort.
*   **Locality Loophole:** In lab-scale trapped-ion or NV-center experiments, measurement stations are separated by < 10 m, allowing classical communication between stations within the measurement time window—invalidating the spacelike separation guarantee.
*   **Measurement Dependence (Freedom-of-Choice) Loophole:** Setting choices are often predetermined or generated by pseudo-random number generators with insufficient entropy, leaving room for an adversary to influence or predict the measurement basis.
*   **Finite-Size Insecurity:** Many protocols lack rigorous finite-key analysis, reporting asymptotic key rates that collapse when realistic block sizes (N < 10⁷) are applied.

**Old Route Ceiling (60 Score Baseline)**
The standard approach is CHSH-based DI-QKD with fair-sampling assumption, using SNSPDs at 70–80% efficiency, measurement stations co-located or separated by < 50 m, and classical RNGs for setting choice. Typical CHSH violation: S = 2.1–2.25. Distance: < 10 m (ion traps) or < 11 km (early fiber). Key rate: 0.001–0.01 bits/pair. Security proof: asymptotic only, no finite-size correction. **The 60 score of the old route has exhausted all degrees of freedom in adjustable parameters—further tuning only reduces efficiency, and further changes require entirely new equipment. Its upper limit is not a technical limitation, but a physical one.**

**1. Breakthrough Solution (What)**
*   **Core Architecture:** A **heralded-entanglement DI-QKD** architecture comprising: (a) two remote node stations (Alice and Bob) each containing a matter qubit (trapped ion or neutral atom) coupled to a cavity or nanofiber for photonic interfacing; (b) a central Bell-state-measurement (BSM) station (Charlie) performing photon interference and heralding; (c) ≥ 2 km fiber spools separating Alice/Bob from Charlie and from each other to guarantee spacelike separation; (d) quantum-proof random number generators (QRNG) at each station for measurement-basis selection; (e) SNSPD arrays with ≥ 90% system detection efficiency covering all output modes.
*   **Physical Path:** Matter qubits are entangled via heralded entanglement swapping—each node emits a photon entangled with its matter qubit; photons travel to Charlie for BSM; a successful BSM heralds the establishment of entanglement between Alice and Bob's matter qubits. Only heralded events enter the Bell test, eliminating detection-loophole post-selection bias.
*   **Verification Mechanism:** CHSH inequality S = ⟨A₀B₀⟩ + ⟨A₀B₁⟩ + ⟨A₁B₀⟩ − ⟨A₁B₁⟩ is evaluated per-round. A running EAT estimator accumulates min-entropy across N rounds, triggering key extraction only when the global Bell violation exceeds the security threshold S_min (collective-attack threshold S_c ≈ 2.12; coherent-attack margin adds ΔS ≈ 0.15).

*   **Detektionsschlupfloch (Fair Sampling):** Die meisten DI-QKD-Demonstrationen nehmen Fair-Sampling an – nur erkannte Ereignisse werden analysiert, was implizit vertraut, dass unerkannte Ereignisse derselben Statistik folgen würden. *   **Finite-Size-Innsicherheit:** Viele Protokolle fehlt eine strenge Finite-Size-Analyse, die asymptotische Schlüsselraten berichten, die zusammenbrechen, wenn realistische Blockgrößen (N < 10⁷) angewendet werden.

*   **Kernarchitektur:** Eine **angekündigte-Verschränkung-DI-QKD**-Architektur, bestehend aus: (a) zwei fernen Knotenstationen (Alice und Bob), die jeweils ein Materie-Qubit (gefangenes Ion oder Neutralatom) enthalten, das an eine Kavität oder Nanofaser für die photonische Schnittstelle gekoppelt ist; (b) einer zentralen Bell-Zustands-Messungs-(BSM)-Station (Charlie), die Photoneninterferenz und Ankündigung durchführt; (c) ≥ 2 km Glasfaserwicklungen, die Alice/Bob von Charlie und voneinander trennen, um raumartige Trennung zu garantieren; (d) quantensichere Zufallszahlengeneratoren (QRNG) an jeder Station für die Wahl der Messbasis; (e) SNSPD-Arrays mit ≥ 90 % Systemdetektionseffizienz, die alle Ausgangsmodi abdecken.
*   **Physikalischer Pfad:** Materie-Qubits werden über angekündigten Verschränkungsaustausch verschränkt – jeder Knoten emittiert ein Photon, das mit seinem Materie-Qubit verschränkt ist; die Photonen reisen zu Charlie für BSM; eine erfolgreiche BSM kündigt die Etablierung der Verschränkung zwischen Alice und Bobs Materie-Qubits an. Nur angekündigte Ereignisse gehen in den Bell-Test ein, wodurch die Auswahlabweichung des Detektionsschlupflochs eliminiert wird.
*   **Verifikationsmechanismus:** Die CHSH-Ungleichung S = ⟨A₀B₀⟩ + ⟨A₀B₁⟩ + ⟨A₁B₀⟩ − ⟨A₁B₁⟩ wird rundenweise ausgewertet. Ein laufender EAT-Schätzer akkumuliert Min-Entropie über N Runden und löst nur dann die SchlüsselexTraktion aus, wenn die globale Bell-Verletzung den Sicherheitsschwellenwert S_min überschreitet (Kollektivangriffs-Schwellenwert S_c ≈ 2,12; Kohärenzangriffs-Marge fügt ΔS ≈ 0,15 hinzu).

**Parametervergleich:**
*   **Detektionseffizienz:** Basis 70–80 % (Fair-Sampling) → Ziel **≥ 90 %** (schlupflochfrei)
*   **CHSH-Wert S:** Basis 2,1–2,25 → Ziel **≥ 2,3** (mit statistischer Signifikanz p < 0,01)
