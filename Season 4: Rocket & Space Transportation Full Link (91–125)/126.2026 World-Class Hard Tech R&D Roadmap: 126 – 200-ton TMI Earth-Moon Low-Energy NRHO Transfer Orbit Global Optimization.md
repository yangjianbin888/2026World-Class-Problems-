The current 60-point solution for TMI (Trans-Lunar Injection) + NRHO insertion suffers from three simultaneous physical deadlocks:

- **Structural deadlock:** Old route has exhausted all free parameters — inclination adjustment, perigee altitude, lunar flyby distance — without reducing total ΔV below 3200 m/s. Further tweaking degrades insertion accuracy; further redesign requires new propulsion stages.
