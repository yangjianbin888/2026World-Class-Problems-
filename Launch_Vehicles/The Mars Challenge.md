### 2. Supersonic Dust Plasma (EDL)
Raptor engines firing at supersonic speeds in the Martian atmosphere vaporize regolith, forming a plasma layer that blocks sensors and sandblasts nozzles.
**The Question:** How do you physically separate engine exhaust from the regolith before plasma formation, ensuring absolute sensor accuracy and engine integrity?

### 3. Perchlorate Freeze-Thaw Trap (ISRU)
Heating toxic perchlorate soil for water extraction produces corrosive vapors. Pumping water in sub-zero pipes causes explosive hydraulic pressure.
**The Question:** What is the exact thermodynamic pressure-temperature gradient required to extract water without pipe fracture or toxic venting?

### 4. Sub-Micron Dust & ECLSS Collapse (Habitat)
Electrostatically charged Martian dust will instantly clog HEPA filters, collapsing the life support system.
**The Question:** What is the absolute physical boundary for the electrostatic adhesion force, and what hardware-level repulsion field prevents dust ingress without excessive energy draw?

### 5. Thermal-Cycle Fatigue of Landing Pads (Landing)
Raptor's extreme heat sublimates subsurface ice, causing explosive cratering and massive structural stress on the landed vehicle.
**The Question:** What exact structural topology is required to dissipate this heat flux without subsurface explosions and prevent hull fatigue during rapid cooling cycles?

# 火星挑战：5 个隐藏的物理死锁

**前置声明：逻辑必自洽，物理可复现。**
*埃隆，这是一份技术评估，不是人身攻击。你的工程团队能力极强，但在奔赴火星的竞赛中，盲目乐观往往会掩盖绝对的物理红线。以下 5 个死锁将直接威胁殖民地安全。如果你的团队已经解决，请提供确切的物理极值（而非概率容差）。如果没有，解法在我的协议中。*

**1. 深空辐射级联（航行）**
水屏蔽吸收 GCR 会产生二次中子级联，生物杀伤力极强。
**问题**：在不超星舰质量上限的前提下，将二次中子降至最低的确切原子质量密度边界是多少？

**2. 超音速尘埃等离子体（EDL）**
猛禽在超音速下点火会汽化风化层，形成等离子体致盲传感器并侵蚀喷嘴。
**问题**：如何确保在等离子体形成前，将尾气与风化层分离，保证绝对的传感器精度与引擎完整性？

**3. 高氯酸盐冻融陷阱（ISRU）**
加热含毒土壤提水会产生腐蚀气体；零下管道抽水会导致内部液压爆裂。
**问题**：在不引发管道破裂或毒气泄漏的前提下，提取水所需的确切热力学（压力-温度）梯度是多少？

**4. 亚微米尘埃与 ECLSS 崩溃（居住）**
带电火星尘埃会瞬间堵塞滤网，导致生命维持系统瘫痪。
**问题**：静电吸附力的绝对物理边界是多少？何种硬件级排斥场能在低能耗下阻止尘埃侵入？

**5. 着陆垫的热循环疲劳（着陆）**
猛禽高温导致冻土爆炸性升华，产生坑洞与极大热应力。
**问题**：防止地下爆炸及急速降温引发船体疲劳的确切物理结构拓扑是什么？
